Local-first means local

Your viewing history stays under your control.

Bynji has no advertising, analytics, telemetry, developer-operated cloud, or user account. Your Library is stored in your browser until you delete or export it.

Compatibility

Made for video across the web.

Bynji works with standard web video players in current Chromium browsers on Windows, macOS and Linux. Website and player behavior varies, so compatibility is continuously improved without relying on a fixed list of supported sites.
